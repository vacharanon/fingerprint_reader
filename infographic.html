Fingerprint Reader – Check-In Guide

Follow these simple steps to check in with the fingerprint reader

0
🪪

Get Your Fingerprint Enrolled

Before you can check in, your fingerprint must be registered in the system. Not enrolled yet? Contact HR and they will add your fingerprint to the reader. This only needs to be done once.

1
✅

Confirm the Device Is Ready

Approach the reader and check its status before placing your finger. The device is ready when you see the signals below.

🖥️  OLED screen:  READY LED: Green (steady)
▼   ▼   ▼
2
👆

Place Your Finger on the Reader

Press your enrolled finger firmly and flatly on the sensor window. Hold it still for 1–2 seconds while the reader scans. Make sure your finger covers the entire sensor surface for the best read.

3
⚠️

Rapid LED Blinking? Lift & Retry

If the LED blinks rapidly, the reader could not capture a clear image. Lift your finger, wait a moment, and try again. Common causes: wet or dirty finger, incorrect positioning, or low pressure.

LED: Rapid blinking – lift & retry
▼   ▼   ▼
4
🎉

Success! You're Checked In

When your fingerprint is recognised, the device confirms your check-in. Your attendance is recorded automatically in the system.

🖥️  OLED screen:  OK LED: Green (steady)
5
❌

Error Message Displayed

If the fingerprint is not recognised (e.g. not enrolled, poor scan quality), the OLED will show a descriptive error message. Read the message carefully — it will guide you on what went wrong.

🖥️  OLED screen:  ERROR MSG LED: Red
♻️ Step 6 – Feel free to retry!  You can check in as many times as you need. There is no limit. Simply place your finger again at any time.
💡 Tips for a good scan:  Keep your finger clean and dry · Press with even, moderate pressure · Centre your fingertip on the sensor · Hold still until the LED responds
Fingerprint Reader Check-In System  ·  Having trouble? Contact HR to verify your enrolment or reset your fingerprint.
